A living kitchen notebook for the recipes that matter.

Tablestock is a mobile app for organizing, versioning, and printing family recipes — built around the idea that a recipe keeps changing across people and generations, and deserves a record that changes with it.

In development · Launching fall 2026

What Tablestock does

Recipe lineage

Every recipe has a family tree — originals, versions, changes, and the people behind each one.

Print-ready cards & books

Turn any version into a printable recipe card or keepsake book — a snapshot of a living archive.

Made for your kitchen

Local-first, no account required. Your recipes live on your device, not on someone else's server.
