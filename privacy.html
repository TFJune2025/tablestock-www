2. Summary

Tablestock is built to be local-first. In its current version, the App does not require you to create an account, does not sync your data to our servers, and does not know who you are unless you contact us directly. Most of what you enter into the App — recipes, notes, photos, versions — stays on your device.

3. Information We Collect

Recipes, recipe notes, versions, photos, tags, and any other content you enter into the App ("User Content") is stored locally on your device. We do not receive, transmit, or store copies of your User Content on any server we control.

3.2 Purchase Information

The App is offered as a one-time paid purchase through the Apple App Store and/or Google Play. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple and Google under their own respective privacy policies and terms. Tablestock does not receive, process, or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

We use the limited information described above only to respond to support requests, diagnose and fix bugs (if diagnostics are enabled), comply with legal obligations, and improve the App generally based on aggregated, non-identifying signals only.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only with Apple and Google (as distribution/payment platforms), service providers who help us operate (if any), and legal authorities if required by law.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
